The middle of the day

Second Pour

Tastes like caramel and a walnut. Rounder and warmer, built for the second cup and for milk if you take it.

How to brew
French press or drip. Coarser, a little more coffee, four minutes.
If we were allowed jargon

A natural Brazil for the sweetness and the weight, a washed Guatemalan for structure, roasted to the point where the sugars have caramelized and the fruit has become something more like a nut. Notes of caramel, walnut, cocoa.

Why this one is for the middle of the day.

The second cup is the working one. It goes in a tumbler, it gets carried, it gets milk, and it has to hold up at two in the afternoon when the first one has worn off. So this one is heavier and sweeter, with nothing bright enough to argue with the milk.

The line on the bag sits halfway. It is the middle of the day, and the middle of the three: if you only ever bought one bag, this would be the one to buy.
