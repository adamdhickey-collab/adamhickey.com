## About

Software that fits the job and the person doing it.

Twenty years across visual design, interactive development and enterprise
product design. I design the systems teams build from, build working versions
with coding agents, and decide what ships.

I started as a hybrid designer and developer, eight years across three
Minneapolis studios, the last five at Pixel Farm, building what I designed in
HTML, CSS and jQuery. Then I spent thirteen years at RBA on large enterprise
products for clients including Cargill, CBRE, Toro, Banner Bank and
International Trucks: 40+ SAP apps brought under one design system and one
accessibility standard, a USDA-backed grant platform that replaced the
spreadsheets it ran on, a dispatch cockpit for a 450+ truck fleet designed on
the floor with the dispatchers, and a global innovation network's identity
carried into its internal platform.

Since RBA I've been building independently with coding agents: Door County
Found and Lucy Learns, two products in use; Faunaform, a screen-print studio
in progress; and a lab of working prototypes, among them Agent Review, a
simulated scenario of a product team where the agents build the screens and
the designer decides what ships.

What I bring to a product team: turning ambiguity into a decision, building
the system everyone builds from, owning the design inside the team, and
building to find out. Visual craft runs through all of it: identity,
illustration and type, by hand.

I'm looking for a senior product design role, in-house, remote in the US or
hybrid in the Twin Cities, with a team that can hire in Minnesota.
