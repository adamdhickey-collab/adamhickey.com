About

Adam Hickey is a senior product designer in Minneapolis, Minnesota, who designs and builds software that has grown complicated: enterprise platforms, design systems, operational workflows, and how people and AI agents work together, after twenty years of studio design and development, then enterprise consulting.

Complicated software is my favorite kind of problem. It took twenty years and two careers to be able to say that.

Design and build · 2005-2013I started as a hybrid designer and developer, eight years across three studios, the last five at Pixel Farm. Designing and building weren’t separate jobs yet. Whoever shipped it sat close enough to argue with.

Systems · 2013-2026Then the jobs split, and I spent thirteen years at RBA on large enterprise products for CBRE, Toro, Banner Bank, and International Trucks. I learned that most design problems are communication problems, and that coherence at scale takes systems.

The longest engagement was building a UX practice inside a global enterprise. We sat with people through their workday and fixed what slowed them. Some of it I explained in public, to a user group with the same problems.

Design and build, with agents · 2026–nowSince RBA I’ve been building independently with coding agents: two products in use, a print studio’s production line, client work, and a lab of working prototypes. The newest follows one shopping trip from a phone into a store.

At RBA I chose the practice over the code. I led the UX work, and developers built from my designs and direction. Coding agents changed that: now I can design and build, and judgment is the scarce part.

What I want next is one team starting something new, early enough that the direction is still open.

The test I still use is one question: when the project ends, is the team better at this than it was?
