Approach

What I bring to a creative team

Four things, in combination. I’m one designer on a team of writers, strategists, marketers, developers and other designers, and this is my part.

  1. Find the creative idea

    Who it’s for, what’s in tension, and the direction worth committing to. The idea has to be sayable in a sentence before it’s drawable, and it has to decide things.

    Seen in Say When and Door County Found.

  2. Build the visual language

    Identity, type, color, illustration and art direction, as a flexible system rather than one composition, so other people can make things with it and it still looks like itself.

    Seen in Innovators Studio, Say When and Faunaform.

  3. Bring the brand into digital

    Sites, interfaces, campaigns and interactive pieces that keep the idea intact on a phone, in a feed and inside a product, with the behavior designed as carefully as the picture.

    Seen in the studio years, Lucy Learns and the Say When storefront.

  4. Refine until it feels finished

    Visual judgment, prototyping and code, with contemporary tools, until the hierarchy, spacing, motion and every state hold together and nothing looks left over.

    Seen in 40+ SAP apps and this site.

Twenty years, three kinds of place: studios where designing and building were one job, enterprise teams where coherence at scale was the job, and independent projects where I’ve done both. The constant is the craft.

About

Adam Hickey is a senior digital designer in Minneapolis, Minnesota: twenty years of brand identity, illustration, visual systems and thoughtfully crafted digital experiences, from three design studios through thirteen years of enterprise product work to independent projects he designs and builds himself.

I started in graphic design, and the thing I still enjoy most is watching an idea turn into a finished visual thing that people use.

Studios · 2005–2013I began as a hybrid graphic designer and front-end developer at ByDesign, LEVEL and Pixel Farm: visually rich sites, interactive experiences, digital campaigns and interfaces for clients like Lund Boats, Fulton Beer and Hazelden. Designing and building were one job, and whoever shipped it sat close enough to argue with. The studio years.

Enterprise · 2013–2026Then thirteen years at RBA on large digital experiences, application suites, operational software and design systems for CBRE, Toro, Banner Bank and International Trucks. The work was clarity, hierarchy and coherence across products that had grown complicated, and it taught me how far a visual system can carry. Some of it I explained in public.

Independent · 2026–nowSince RBA I’ve made things end to end: a travel brand for my sister, a training app for our dog, a screen-print studio, a book, and a fictional coffee brand to show the whole arc. I use coding agents and image models the way I used to use jQuery, as tools that make the visual work real faster. The judgment is mine.

What I want next is a senior craft role on a team where visual excellence matters.

The test I still use is one question: when the work ships, does it look like someone cared about every part of it?
