# Content decisions, page by page

What happened to every page of the site in the repositioning of 2026-10-26,
in one table. The decisions are rebuilt (new page on the storytelling
system), rewritten (same page, new story), retained with minor changes
(titles, kickers, a paragraph), demoted (kept and reachable, moved out of the
first impression) and redirected (the address stays, the content moved).
The why is one line. The audit behind the decisions is `site-audit.md`; the
flagship and the three rewritten case studies have their own notes.

## Work

| Page | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `case-study/say-when.html` | New | The flagship: a self-initiated fictional coffee brand carried from strategy to a storefront. Labeled fictional everywhere it appears. |
| `case-study/innovators-studio-visual-identity.html` | Rebuilt | The key credibility piece: real multidisciplinary identity work in a professional setting, told with the identity as the protagonist. |
| `case-study/door-county-found.html` | Rewritten | An independent travel brand with an editorial identity, told identity first; the technology is a short proof near the end. |
| `case-study/lucy-learns.html` | Rewritten | Visual communication that keeps attention on the dog; the three tries become a story about watching a real household. |
| `case-study/hybrid-designer.html` | Rebuilt | The studio years were a list of eight equal cards on the oldest template. They are the origin of the visual craft the site now leads with, so they moved onto the storytelling system with the work shown large and every claim kept at the level the old page made. |
| `case-study/sap-product-maturity.html` | Retained with minor changes | The scale and the numbers stay; the description and the solution now say what the contribution was: one visual language, one hierarchy, a component that means the same thing on every screen. Not recast as branding. |
| `case-study/usda-operational-overhead.html` | Retained with minor changes | Same template, same facts; the framing is clarity and communicating complex information. |
| `case-study/dispatch-complexity.html` | Retained with minor changes | The craft was in the cards, and the opening says so; its foot now continues to the enterprise years rather than the prototype, so the enterprise chain reads as one and ends on the studio years. |
| `case-study/enterprise-consulting.html` | Retained with minor changes | One paragraph names the visual job the thirteen years had in common. Depth rather than first impression. |
| `other.html` | Rebuilt | "Other things I make" held the book and a shelf of identity pieces. It is now the full work index, one row per page, so nothing is reachable only by knowing it is there. The address stays because it was linked. |
