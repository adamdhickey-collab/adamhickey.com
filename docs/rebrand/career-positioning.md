# Career positioning

The public half of the positioning work: the role the site is written for
and what makes the work distinctive. It is the brief every page's copy
answers, and it says nothing that is not already on the site.

## The role

Senior Digital Designer, Senior Visual Designer, Digital Art Director, Brand
Experience Designer, Interactive Designer: a senior craft role as an
individual contributor on a team of writers, strategists, marketers,
developers and other designers, at an agency, a multidisciplinary studio or
an in-house group where visual excellence matters. Also a fit for an
organization that wants an experienced interface designer with exceptional
visual craft. In Minneapolis, hybrid or remote.

## What makes the work distinctive

- **Twenty years of the same craft in three kinds of place.** Studios where
  designing and building were one job (ByDesign, LEVEL, Pixel Farm, 2005 to
  2013); enterprise teams where coherence at scale was the job (RBA, 2013 to
  2026); independent projects where both happen at once (2026 on).
- **The idea comes first and decides things.** Door County Found's identity
  came out of "advice from someone who knows the place"; Say When's whole
  system came out of the pour. The site shows the idea, then the visual
  decisions it made, then how the experience works.
- **A visual language built as a system.** Innovators Studio's identity,
  illustration, badges and templates were made so other people could make
  things with them; Say When's three bags are a family by a rule rather than
  a recolor.
- **The brand survives the trip into digital.** Lucy Learns, Door County
  Found and the Say When storefront are working products whose screens look
  like the brand they belong to, and the SAP work is the same discipline at
  enterprise scale.
- **Finished means finished.** Hierarchy, spacing, motion and every state
  hold together, and the site itself is held to that by eleven checks that
  measure the rendered page.
- **Technical fluency that serves the visual work.** HTML, CSS, JavaScript,
  prototyping, design systems and AI-assisted production are how the ideas
  get made real, not the headline.

## What the positioning leaves out, on purpose

AI expertise as a headline, product strategy, executive or management
ambition, compensation, and any brand-identity claim beyond the history on
the page. The coffee brand is labeled a self-initiated fictional concept
wherever it appears, and nothing on the site is invented.

## Where the message lives

The homepage headline and its supporting line, the four abilities under
Approach, the About section's three beats, the contact invitation, the
Person in the site's structured data, `llms.txt`, and the two résumés
(`scripts/resume.mjs`). The wording of any profile outside this repository
is a separate decision and is not made here.
