# Positioning

The one-page brief every page of the repositioned site is written to.
Written 2026-10-26, with the archive of the previous site in
`recovery.md`.

## What changed

The previous site positioned Adam as a senior product designer for
complicated enterprise software, and later as someone who supervises AI
coding agents. Both were true of recent work and neither is the designer
he is at his best. The site now leads with what he has done for twenty
years and enjoys most: finding a visual idea and bringing it to life across
brand, digital and interactive work, to a very high standard of finish.

## The headline

**Visual ideas, beautifully brought to life.**

Supporting line: *I'm a Minneapolis-based senior digital designer with
twenty years of experience creating distinctive visual systems and
thoughtfully crafted digital experiences.*

## Who it is for

Hiring managers and creative directors at agencies, multidisciplinary
studios and in-house teams where visual excellence matters, and
organizations that want an experienced interface designer with
exceptional visual craft. The site is a destination for that reader, not
a directory of services.

## The four abilities

Every major project on the site shows some combination of these. The claim
is a credible combination, not expertise at everything.

1. **Find the creative idea.** Audience, tension or opportunity, creative
   direction.
2. **Build the visual language.** Identities, type, illustration, color,
   art direction, flexible systems.
3. **Bring the brand into digital.** Websites, interfaces, campaigns,
   interactive experiences.
4. **Refine until it feels finished.** Visual judgment, prototyping, code,
   contemporary tools, a cohesive result.

## What the site does not say

- It does not lead with AI expertise, product strategy, executive
  ambition or compensation.
- It does not call enterprise consulting what defines him. Those years
  expanded the work; the technical fluency they built is what makes the
  visual work effective.
- It claims no brand-identity expertise beyond the history on the page,
  and labels the coffee brand a self-initiated fictional concept wherever
  it appears.
- It invents nothing: no research, clients, approvals, results, awards,
  sales or print runs.

## The standard

Within 30 seconds a creative director should see an experienced visual
thinker who develops a distinctive idea, brings it into digital and
refines it to a very high standard. Within five minutes, the proof. By the
end, that the technical background expands what he can create rather than
defining it.
