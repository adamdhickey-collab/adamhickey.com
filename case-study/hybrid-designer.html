8 years as a hybrid designer / developer (2005-2013)

Employers

My role
Interactive designer / developer
Timeline
2005-2013

Overview

For eight years I worked at three Minneapolis studios, the last five at Pixel Farm. I built interactive websites and apps with some warmth to them, turning corporate ideas into something people would actually want to spend time with.

The internet is a noisy place. Everyone is shouting. Our job was to be the ones whispering something interesting in the corner, taking big corporate ideas and making them feel human.

Sometimes we succeeded. Sometimes we just made really nice-looking buttons. But we always tried to tell a story.
